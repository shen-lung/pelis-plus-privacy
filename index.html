Privacy Policy for Pelis+

Last Updated: November 2026

Thank you for using Pelis+. Your privacy is important to us. This application provides information about movies currently playing in theaters, popular movies, top-rated movies, and upcoming releases.

Information We Collect

Pelis+ does not request, collect, store, or share any personal information from users. The application does not require registration, account creation, or authentication to be used.

Application Usage

The application allows users to browse public movie information and save favorite movies locally on their device to improve the overall user experience.

Favorites

Movies marked as favorites are stored exclusively on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to external servers and is not shared with third parties.

Permissions

Pelis+ does not request sensitive permissions and does not access personal information such as contacts, location, camera, microphone, or private files stored on the device.

Third-Party Services

The application may retrieve public movie information from external services specialized in movie and entertainment content. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The application is designed for a general audience and does not collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application or to comply with legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the email address provided on Google Play.

TMDB Attribution

Some of the content displayed in Pelis+ is provided by The Movie Database (TMDB), a community-built database of movie and television information.

Pelis+ uses the TMDB API to retrieve and display public information related to movies, including now playing titles, popular movies, top-rated movies, upcoming releases, ratings, descriptions, images, and associated media assets.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

For more information, please review TMDB's Privacy Policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
